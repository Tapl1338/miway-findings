# Just-miss detector — minimum schedule shifts per pairing

> **Lineage:** generated by the just-miss detector (`just_miss.md` /
> `just_miss.csv` in the analysis home) — scheduled-time transfer analysis
> at Meadowvale Town Centre terminal. Vintage: 2026-09-04. Promoted to the
> findings lane 2026-09-11.
>
> **What this adds:** the councillor brief said "shifting a few departures
> by 3–5 minutes can fix specific pairings." This is the complete ranked
> evidence: every pairing, every miss, and exactly how many misses each
> candidate shift converts — the quantitative engine behind the transfer
> ask.

## Method (from the source header)

Stop filter: `Meadowvale Town Centre Bus Terminal` · buffer 2 min · miss
threshold 15 min wait · candidate shifts [3, 5, 8] min · rider weighting:
boardings CSV · platforms: pooled into one terminal node · substitute pairs:
demoted (corridor overlap).

`exit_ratio`/`divergence` (CSV) measure the pair's shared corridor out of
the terminal (first ~12 stops beyond the terminal access stem). Ratio ≥ 0.25
means the connection does not happen at this terminal: substitutes
(express/local twins) or a transfer that relocates to where the routes
diverge. Their gain is scaled by (1 − exit_ratio).

A *miss* = an A-departure whose next B departure leaves more than the
miss-threshold later. *Conversions* = misses a shift of that size turns
into catches of the same next B bus. Ranked by conversions per shifted
minute.

**Network summary:** 4,949 missed departures across 156 pairings; a
3-minute shift network-wide converts **1,347** of them; 114 pairings have
at least one convertible miss.

Caveats: scheduled-time analysis — delays can still break a re-timed
pairing (see `realized_waits.py` for the simulated-real side). Shifts apply
to one route's departure block; dwell/layover constraints are not modeled.
Candidate shifts are fixed-size; a planner would optimize per pairing.
