# Realized vs scheduled transfer waits — Meadowvale Town Centre

> **Lineage:** generated by `backend/scripts/realized_waits.py` — feeds the
> terminal's timetable through the collector's observed lateness
> (`obs_lateness.csv`, 688,374 near-real-time rows; seed date 2026-08-23,
> single-timetable filter 2026-08-18 service block) and simulates every
> transfer the schedule promises, then every transfer that actually
> happens once buses run as observed. Vintage: 2026-09-04. Promoted to the
> findings lane 2026-09-11.
>
> **What this adds:** the just-miss analysis is *scheduled-time*; this is
> the **realized** side — what lateness does to the same pairings once the
> buses actually run.

**Departure-weighted mean wait: scheduled ≈ 13.5 min → realized ≈ 14.1
min.** The average hides the tail; the pairings below are where lateness
concentrates.

Note the 90 → 42 case: a pairing whose *scheduled* mean wait is already the
best on the board (4.2 min) loses almost three minutes of that advantage to
lateness — sync quality and lateness are separable problems, and fixing one
does not fix the other.

Caveats: departure time stands in for alight time on terminal platforms;
lateness pools are route-wide (not stop-specific); school specials included
in the feed pattern.
