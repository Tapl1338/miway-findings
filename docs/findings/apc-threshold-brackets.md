`a – b+` = threshold is above a and at most b. For down-flips (`a – b`, no
plus) the threshold lies between b and a.

Across 13 vehicles with brackets, **41 of 78 cross-vehicle bracket pairs
overlap** — overlap means two vehicles' thresholds are consistent with each
other; non-overlap is direct evidence thresholds differ per vehicle. That is
the statistical basis for "thresholds differ per vehicle" in the brief: it
is not an impression, it is 37 non-overlapping bracket pairs.

## 2. Display lag after a real load change

- Stale-display events (≥4 pax moved, bucket unmoved): **40**
- Caught up before observations ended: 29 (never: 11)
- Median catch-up lag: **4.7 min** (p90 14.5 min)

A four-passenger change going invisible for a median of ~5 minutes means a
stop-level load sample taken at one moment can be stale by a full stop or
two of boarding activity — relevant to anyone consuming APC feeds for
service planning.
